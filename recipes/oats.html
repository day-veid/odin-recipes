Oats Recipe

description

This recipe features a favorite nigeria dish for me personally, it's easy to make and can be made differently according to prefrence

Ingredients

  • small amount of oats
  • half cup of milk
  • tablespoon of brown sugar
  • 2 bananas

Steps to Prepare

  1. in a small bowl add your oats
  2. add equal amount of water and mix
  3. microwave for 2 minutes
  4. add half cup of milk and sugar then stir
  5. microwave for another 1 minute
  6. slice bananas to decorate and enjoy
