Bread and Egg Recipe

description

This recipe features a favorite nigeria dish for me personally, it's easy to make and can be made differently according to prefrence

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2-3 fresh eggs
  • pepper
  • salt
  • maggi
  • onions
  • sardines

Steps to Prepare

  1. in a small bowl crack all your eggs
  2. whisk them together and add all listed spices
  3. chop onions and add as well
  4. add sardines and whisk all together
  5. fry in little oil till well done on both sides/li>
  6. serve on the bread and enjoy!!
