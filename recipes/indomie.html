Indomie Recipe

description

This recipe features a favorite nigeria dish for all, it's easy to make and can be made differently according to prefrence

Ingredients

  • 2 super pack indomie
  • 2 fresh eggs
  • pepper
  • onions
  • sardines

Steps to Prepare

  1. in a small pot add enough water to boil your 2 eggs for 13 minutes
  2. in another small pot add the 2 super packs and add some water to boil
  3. add included seasoning into the indomie
  4. chop onions and add as well
  5. add sardines and a table spoon of pepper
  6. stir and allow cook for 5 minutes
  7. serve and enjoy!!
